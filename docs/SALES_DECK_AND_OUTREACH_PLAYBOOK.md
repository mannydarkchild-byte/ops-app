This playbook provides the exact scripts, slides, outreach sequences, and objection-handling strategies to package and sell **OPS** as a high-margin B2B product to plant hire companies, quarrying operators, mining contractors, and civil earthmoving fleets.

## 1. Target Buyer Personas

In heavy equipment operations, you typically sell to one of three stakeholders:

### A. The Plant Hire Managing Director / Business Owner
- **Primary Pain:** Month-end invoice write-offs and client disputes over billable machine hours. Cash flow delays caused by disputed downtime claims.
- **What they care about:** Cash flow, legal/contractual protection, invoice acceptance, and proof of work.
- **Your Hook:** *"Eliminate month-end billing disputes and recover 10 to 30 lost billable hours per machine every month with supervisor-signed digital shift slips."*

### B. The Quarry / Site Operations Manager
- **Primary Pain:** Constant chaos between machine operators, mechanics, and dispatchers. Paper logbooks smeared with grease or lost. No idea what happened during the night shift.
- **What they care about:** Machine availability, productivity, safety compliance (pre-starts), and avoiding production bottlenecks.
- **Your Hook:** *"One live pulse of your entire site — pre-starts, real running time vs idle stops, fuel burn, and breakdown work orders — even with zero cellular signal in the pit."*

### C. The Fleet Maintenance Director / Workshop Lead
- **Primary Pain:** Unreported machine faults turning into catastrophic failures (e.g. blown hydraulic pumps, seized bearings). Missing parts from the storeroom.
- **What they care about:** Preventive maintenance, repair job tracking, parts inventory control, and machine service history.
- **Your Hook:** *"Close the loop between the operator cab and the workshop. Unreported rattles become mechanic work orders before they shut down your site."*

## 4. The 15-Minute Live Demo Script (Step-by-Step)

When you get a prospect on a screen share (or in person on a phone):

### Step 1: The Hook (2 Minutes)
*"Thanks for taking the time, [Name]. Before I show you the app, tell me: when you submit your monthly invoices to the mine or client, how often do they challenge you on machine hours or downtime?"*
*(Listen actively. They will tell you their exact horror stories about lost money).*

### Step 2: The Operator Experience (4 Minutes)
Open the **Operator App** on mobile view:
1. Show the **Pre-start Checklist**: *"Your operator walks around the machine. They check the engine oil, hydraulic hoses, and emergency stop. They can use voice-to-text to speak a defect in 5 seconds."*
2. Show the **Hour Meter Photo**: *"They snap a photo of the opening meter: 1,284.6 hours. The photo is locked to the shift. No more pencil estimates."*
3. Show **Stopping the Machine**: *"The machine stops. The operator hits 'Stop Machine'. They pick the reason: 'Waiting for Loader'. The app immediately marks that downtime as SITE OWNED. That means you still get paid for those hours."*

### Step 3: The Supervisor Sign-Off (3 Minutes)
Switch to the **Supervisor App**:
1. *"At the end of the shift, the site supervisor opens the shift. They see the opening and closing meter photos, total run time, and why the machine stopped."*
2. *"They review it, accept it, and sign right on the glass with their finger. Once signed, it's locked. The client can never dispute those hours again."*

### Step 4: The Executive View & WhatsApp Summary (3 Minutes)
1. Show the **Productivity Pulse**: *"From your desk at head office, you see every machine's pulse line across all sites."*
2. Show the **WhatsApp Shift Report**: *"At 17:30, your management WhatsApp group gets a clean summary: hours worked, diesel used, stops recorded, and a link to the signed PDF."*

### Step 5: The Close: The 30-Day Pilot (3 Minutes)
*"We don't expect you to take our word for it. We want to put OPS on 2 or 3 of your machines for 30 days. We'll set up your sites in 48 hours and train your team. If it doesn't save you more than its cost in protected billable hours, you don't pay a cent. Can we start setup next Tuesday?"*

## 5. Master Objection Handling Guide

| Objection | Why They Say It | How to Overcome It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Our operators aren't tech-savvy and will break the phones."** | Fear of operator resistance or device damage. | *"We designed the operator screen specifically for operators with zero computer skills: big buttons, high-contrast text, step-by-step guidance, and voice recording. It runs on any cheap R1,500 ($80) rugged Android phone with a rubber case. If they can use WhatsApp to text their family, they can use OPS in under 5 minutes."* |
| **"We have zero cellular signal in our pit/quarry."** | Experience with cloud apps failing in remote locations. | *"That's exactly why we built OPS as an offline-first PWA. All meter photos, shift logs, and supervisor signatures save locally inside the phone's memory using IndexedDB. The operator doesn't need a single bar of signal. As soon as the supervisor or operator walks back to the site office or reaches Wi-Fi, it syncs automatically."* |
| **"We already have GPS telematics (CAT ProductLink / Komtrax)."** | Believing telematics solve shift verification. | *"Telematics are great for telling you where an engine is and if it's running, but they can't tell you WHY it stopped under your commercial contract. Telematics can't tell you if the machine stopped because the screenbox seized or because the client's loader was 2 hours late. Telematics don't take pre-start walkaround defect photos or capture the client supervisor's digital signature. OPS turns machine activity into legally dispute-proof invoices."* |
| **"Our clients will never agree to sign a phone on site."** | Cynicism about client cooperation. | *"In practice, site supervisors actually prefer this over messy paper logbooks because it takes them 30 seconds, they can see the meter photo directly on the screen, and they get an instant PDF copy sent to their email or WhatsApp. It protects them from being blamed for missing logs just as much as it protects you."* |
| **"We already use Excel / paper logbooks and it works fine."** | Complacency with existing habits. | *"Paper logbooks feel free, but they are the most expensive tool in your business. When paper gets lost, greased over, or disputed at month end, you write off tens of thousands in billable hours. OPS costs less than 1 billable machine hour per month, and protects hundreds of thousands in cash flow."* |

## 6. Closing Checklist

When a client agrees to a pilot:
1. Collect their **Machine Roster**: Machine Name, Model (e.g. Powerscreen Warrior 2100), Machine Code/Serial, Current Hour Meter reading.
2. Collect their **Site Information**: Site Name, Client Name, Shift Start/End times, Standard Shift Length (e.g. 8h or 10h).
3. Review their **Contractual Downtime Rules**: What is mechanical (contractor fault) vs wear vs site delay?
4. Send the **B2B Proposal & Pilot Agreement** (`docs/B2B_SALES_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md`) for digital signature.
5. Create their user logins and conduct a 15-minute remote or on-site supervisor walkthrough.
